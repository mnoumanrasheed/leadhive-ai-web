Company information

LeadHive AI ("LeadHive AI", "we", "us", or "our") provides a web-based service that helps YouTube channel owners manage comments and generate or publish AI-assisted replies. This Privacy Policy explains how we access, use, store, protect, share, and delete personal information, including information obtained through Google APIs and YouTube API Services. We access Google account or YouTube data only after you grant the applicable permissions. You can use this policy to understand and control those permissions.

By using LeadHive AI, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy.

1. Google and YouTube Information We Access

When you sign in with Google or connect a YouTube channel, and only within the permissions you grant, LeadHive AI may receive the following information to operate the features you select:

  • Google account information: Your account email address and basic profile information, such as name and profile picture, when made available by the approved sign-in permissions.
  • YouTube channel information: Channel identifier, name, and relevant channel details required to identify the account connected to LeadHive AI.
  • Video information: Identifiers, titles, and other relevant metadata for videos associated with the connected channel.
  • YouTube comments and replies: Comment identifiers, comment text, timestamps, available commenter names or public profile information, reply threads, and reply status needed for comment management.
  • Authorization information: OAuth access and refresh tokens or related technical credentials required to maintain the connection and make authorized API requests. We do not ask for or store your Google or YouTube password.

The exact information available depends on the Google permissions you approve and the features currently enabled in the product. We do not request unrelated Google account data.

2. How LeadHive AI Uses Google User Data

We use the information described above only to provide the services you request, including:

  • Authentication and connection: Signing you in, verifying your connected YouTube channel, and keeping the authorized account connection functioning.
  • Comment management: Retrieving and displaying comments associated with your YouTube videos so you can review and manage them.
  • AI-assisted reply generation: Using the relevant comment text and limited video/channel context to prepare suggested responses tailored to your instructions.
  • Reply publication: Publishing replies to YouTube only with your authorization, either after your approval or under an automatic-reply configuration that you deliberately enable. The available workflow and controls depend on your chosen settings.
  • Service operation and security: Saving relevant settings, preventing misuse, diagnosing failures, and maintaining the reliable operation of authorized features.

We do not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to these user-facing features without first updating our disclosures and obtaining any additional consent required.

3. AI Processing, Service Providers, and Sharing

AI reply-processing provider: OpenAI, L.L.C. Under OpenAI's API data-usage policies, data submitted through the API is not used to train or improve AI models. To generate reply suggestions, we may send this provider the specific comment text and limited contextual information necessary to prepare the requested response. This information is processed on our behalf to deliver the feature, subject to appropriate data-protection and provider-use restrictions.

We may also use infrastructure, hosting, database, and security service providers that process information on our behalf to operate LeadHive AI. We limit their access to information needed for their contracted services and require appropriate confidentiality and security safeguards. When you authorize a reply, relevant reply content is transmitted to YouTube through its APIs to post it to the connected channel.

We do not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers, data brokers, or information resellers. Other disclosures may occur only where necessary to comply with applicable law or to protect the security of users and the service, consistent with Google API policies.

4. Limited Use and Prohibited Uses

LeadHive AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, targeted marketing based on that data, ad personalization, credit or lending decisions, or surveillance. We do not sell Google user data, supply it to data brokers, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. We do not permit our AI-processing providers to use Google user data supplied by LeadHive AI to train generalized models. These commitments apply to both raw Google API data and information derived from it.

5. How We Protect Your Information

LeadHive AI uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss. These safeguards include encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for information in transit, encryption of stored OAuth tokens at rest, restricted access to production systems, role-based access where appropriate, and controls for monitoring or responding to security issues. Access to Google user data is limited to authorized systems and personnel who need it for an approved purpose. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention, Refresh, and Deletion

We retain Google account and YouTube information only for as long as needed to operate the features you have authorized, subject to the retention and refresh requirements of YouTube API Services. Where applicable, stored YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 calendar days rather than retained indefinitely without validation.

  • When you disconnect within LeadHive AI: We stop authorized access, revoke the relevant connection token promptly, and delete the related stored authorized YouTube data as soon as practicable, no later than seven (7) calendar days after the disconnection.
  • When you request deletion or delete your LeadHive AI account: We delete the corresponding stored YouTube data as soon as practicable and no later than seven (7) calendar days after the request, subject to appropriate verification of the requester.
  • When you revoke access through Google: We stop using the revoked authorization and delete Google/YouTube API data associated with it as soon as practicable and within the applicable YouTube API policy deadline, which is no later than 30 calendar days for this revocation route.

To request deletion, contact support@leadhive-ai.com using the email associated with your account. Deleting information held by LeadHive AI does not delete comments, videos, or other content stored directly on YouTube; you must manage that content through YouTube or another authorized tool.

7. Your Choices and How to Revoke Access

You can control or withdraw LeadHive AI’s access to your Google and YouTube information in either of the following ways:

  • Inside LeadHive AI: Choose the Disconnect control for your linked YouTube channel in your account-connection settings.
  • Through Google: Visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove LeadHive AI from the apps or services with access to your Google Account.

If you cannot access your LeadHive AI account, email support@leadhive-ai.com to request assistance with disconnection and deletion. Removing access prevents further authorized API requests using the revoked permission. It does not by itself remove replies already posted to YouTube.

8. Other Information and Essential Website Technologies

When you use our website or application, we may process information you provide directly, such as account preferences and messages to support. We may also process limited technical information, such as application events, browser or device details, and security logs, for sign-in, essential operation, and fraud prevention.

Essential cookies (such as our secure HttpOnly authentication cookie) are used strictly to maintain login sessions and protect against cross-site request forgery. These activities are separate from our restricted use of data received from Google APIs.
